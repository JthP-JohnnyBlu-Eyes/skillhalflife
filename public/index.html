    Why now

    This isn't a someday problem.

    AI doesn't replace a job in one go. It takes over tasks, one at a time, and the skills behind those tasks lose their value. The people who adapt early get to choose where they go next. The people who wait often end up adapting on someone else's terms.

    What is a skill half-life?

    Every skill loses value. Some much faster than others.

    A half-life is how long it takes for something to lose half its strength. Skills work the same way. The ones tied to tasks AI can do quickly, like drafting, summarizing and data entry, fade fastest. Judgment, people skills and hands-on work last much longer.

    The good news: most of what you know still counts. A plan helps you move it toward the work that lasts, and learn the few things that close the gap.

    Your situation

    Administrative assistants score 64 out of 100 on the Job Exposure Index. AI could speed up 11 of the job's 32 tasks on its own and 18 more with extra software. The tasks you said take most of your time, drafting correspondence and keeping calendars, are among the most exposed.

    Recommended direction: project coordination

    You already coordinate schedules, chase follow-ups and keep several people informed. Those are the core of a project coordinator role. Project management specialists score 55, lower than your current job, and the work leans on judgment and people, which AI speeds up less.

    Skills that carry over

    • Scheduling across several people and deadlines
    • Written communication with managers and outside contacts
    • Keeping records, trackers and shared files in order

    Gaps to close

    • Project planning basics: scope, milestones, risks and status reporting

    • A small finished project you can point to in an interview

    When does it open?

    We're building it now and opening it to the waitlist first. Join with your email and we'll tell you as soon as it's ready.

    Does a high score mean I'll lose my job?

    No. Exposure means AI could change how the work is done, not that the job disappears. Most exposed jobs change shape: less time on routine tasks, more on the rest. The plan helps you move toward the part that grows.

    Why pay when free career tools exist?

    Free tools like My Next Move are good for exploring jobs. Skill Half Life combines your specific tasks, your time and budget, an explained comparison of your options, and a schedule in one plan you can act on. You'll see a free preview before you decide.

    Where does the data come from?

    The same public sources as the Job Exposure Index: the US Department of Labor's O*NET job database, Bureau of Labor Statistics data, published research on AI exposure, and the Anthropic Economic Index. Each plan lists its sources and their dates.

    Is a person going to review my plan?

    No. Plans are generated automatically and checked by software: every course, cost and source must come from our approved catalog, and budgets and study hours are checked before the plan is released. If we can't produce a complete plan for your situation, we won't charge you.
